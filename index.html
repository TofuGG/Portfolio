Who I am

I'm Tofu — a CSE undergrad and mostly self-taught developer. I got into coding by curiosity, stayed for the craft, and I'm currently studying Computer Science & Engineering at East West University.

My main goal is game development. I love the idea of building worlds people can get lost in. GDScript and Godot are my playground for that, but I pick up whatever tool the problem calls for.

I'm also interested in cybersecurity — understanding how systems break is just as interesting as building them. Currently learning Java and AutoCAD this semester and adding them to the stack.

Most of what I know, I taught myself. I learn by doing — by shipping things, breaking them, and figuring out why.
